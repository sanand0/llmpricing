LLM Pricing

How fast is the cost of intelligence falling?

For each labelled intelligence level, this plots the lowest input price ever offered by a model with a higher overall Elo score.

Hover near a curve to inspect the model that set its price frontier at that point.

Method. A model qualifies only when its overall Elo is strictly greater than the labelled threshold. Prices are dollars per million input tokens. Blank, zero, or invalid prices are excluded.

Caveat. The education labels are analogies for compressed LMArena Elo bands, not literal academic equivalence.
